# Expansion model boundary

This model separates source provenance, worlds, works, reading units, progress, and spoiler gates.

## Editorial boundary

`verified-index` means metadata is supported by a verified index. `declared-private` records a private source without claiming that its internal text was inspected.

The model does not infer world membership from titles, filenames, or narrative knowledge. Reading units are explicit metadata and must not be used to invent chapter structures.

Spoiler gates are fail-closed: missing, duplicate, cross-work, or unknown requirements remain locked.

The supplied bibliography may be represented as metadata only. Protected EPUB text is not stored or copied by this layer.
