# EXPANSION RUN 101

## Provenance boundary

This iteration adds a reusable provenance audit for future world, work and
reading-content expansion.

A source declaration is not treated as permission to infer editorial content:

- **verified-index** may support bibliographic metadata.
- **declared-private** is catalog information only and supports no editorial
  claim unless the relevant material is independently accessible.
- **editorial-accessible** may support explicit reading structure and original
  summaries when marked accessible.

The module stores no protected passages and does not inspect, reproduce or
derive narrative facts from private EPUBs. Duplicate claim identifiers are
rejected rather than merged, keeping the policy fail-closed.

This boundary can be reused for Scadrial, Sel, Nalthis, Taldain, Roshar and
additional worlds without assigning works, chapters or narrative facts by
title alone.
