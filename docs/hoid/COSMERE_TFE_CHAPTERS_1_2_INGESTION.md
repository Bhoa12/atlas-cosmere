## Original summaries staged for review

### Chapter 1 — Vin

Vin is introduced as a guarded young member of a Luthadel thieves' gang. Her distrust is shaped by her brother Reen's betrayal. The gang leader Camon prepares a fraud in which he poses as an impoverished noble seeking a Ministry transport contract. Vin notices that the servants and presentation do not fit the claimed financial situation and proposes changes. She also uses an unexplained ability she calls Suerte to calm Camon and subtly reduce a prelate's suspicions. The chapter establishes her observational skill, resilience and discreet influence without explaining the source of that ability.

Draft ID: tfe.chapter-001.vin

### Chapter 2 — Kelsier

Kelsier returns to Luthadel and meets Dockson to assemble a team for an ambitious job whose objective is not yet fully disclosed. They discuss former crew members and possible specialists. In parallel, Vin accompanies Camon to the Ministry and discovers his intention to betray Theron and keep the proceeds of the operation. A meeting with the senior prelate Arriev results in a transport contract after Vin again influences the official's emotions. Kelsier and Dockson observe the outcome; Kelsier recognizes signs of Allomancy in Vin and considers that she may be a Mistborn. When a Steel Inquisitor appears, Kelsier distracts it while Dockson handles the pursuers. The chapter brings the Vin and Kelsier threads into proximity without asserting anything beyond chapter 2.

Draft ID: tfe.chapter-002.kelsier
