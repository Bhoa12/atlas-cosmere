# Cosmere expansion run 51

This layer separates source provenance, worlds, works, reading units, progress, and spoiler gates.

## Editorial boundary

- `verified-index` records metadata supported by the verified index supplied for `cosmere.epub`.
- `declared-private` records the existence of a private source without claiming its internal passages were inspected.
- World assignments are optional and are never inferred from titles or filenames.
- Reading units are empty until explicit, supported structural metadata is available.
- The catalogue stores no protected passages or full-text EPUB content.
- Any future synopsis must be original and backed by accessible material; inaccessible passages must not be treated as consulted.

## Spoiler policy

`canReveal` fails closed. A gate requires a matching work, at least one explicit requirement, no duplicate requirements, existing units belonging to the same work, and completion of every required unit.
